# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams reviewing Tea Vercelli's applications for Business Analyst roles.

## Product Purpose

Present Tea's professional profile, academic background, developing skills and completed projects as evidence for job applications. Success means a recruiter can quickly understand her background, current work, analytical approach and practical project experience.

## Positioning

A personal portfolio and work sample, not a commercial website, services page or software product.

## Operating Context

Recruiters arrive from job applications and need to scan profile, contact details, skills and case studies. The project is served locally or as a static website. The Global Superstore dashboard fetches its CSV and therefore needs HTTP rather than file:// access.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio uses separate static pages for the project index and case studies.
- The Global Superstore dashboard reads orders_clean.csv and computes interactive metrics in browser JavaScript.
- global_superstore_analysis.sql is a separate, existing SQL analysis file; it is not called by the dashboard.
- Verified full-dataset dashboard figures: sales $12,642,501.91, profit $1,467,457.29 and profit margin 11.61%.
- Power BI, Python, SQL/Excel and AI automation pages are future project placeholders, not completed projects.
- Use only facts supported by Tea's CV or existing project files; do not invent impact, experience, expertise or results.

## Brand Commitments

- Use English throughout the public website.
- Keep the tone personal, clear and credible for job applications.
- Preserve Tea Vercelli's supplied name, photo, email and profile URLs.
- Do not turn the portfolio into a sales or product-marketing site.

## Product Principles

- Make the professional profile and real project evidence easy to find.
- Distinguish observed results from interpretation and follow-up questions.
- Describe technical skills at the level supported by current evidence.
- Keep future projects visibly separate from completed work.
- Preserve simple static hosting and working local navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus, descriptive image text, readable contrast and layouts that work with mobile viewports and text scaling.
