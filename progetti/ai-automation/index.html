InvoiceFlow.

A local-first workflow that reads supplier invoice PDFs, validates required fields and routes each document to the next operational action.

01PROCESS

From inbox noise to an actionable queue.

The workflow is designed around the operational exception, not around the model. Standard invoices can move forward; anything uncertain is explained, prioritised and routed to a person.

  1. IngestRead a PDF from a local inbox folder.
  2. ExtractCapture supplier, amount, PO and line items.
  3. ValidateMatch the invoice against purchase-order data.
  4. RouteApprove, reject or send to human review.

03CONTROLS

Business rules before AI.

The first layer is deterministic. The local AI layer only adds a human-readable explanation and suggested action; it does not post invoices or bypass approval thresholds.

  • Duplicate checkSame supplier and invoice number cannot be processed twice.
  • Three-way checkInvoice amount and quantity are compared with the purchase order and receipt.
  • Confidence gateLow extraction confidence always routes to review.
  • Audit trailEvery status change stores the actor, timestamp and reason.
04BUILD

Free and local by design.

The intended build uses open-source tools and synthetic data: n8n self-hosted for orchestration, Python for extraction and rules, Tesseract for OCR, Ollama for local AI explanation, SQLite for persistence and this lightweight interface for the operator queue.
